## 🛠️ Activation Troubleshooting

### Office Shows "Unlicensed Product"

Try the following:

1. Check your internet connection.
2. Open Word → **File → Account**.
3. Sign out of Office.
4. Sign in again with the licensed Microsoft account.
5. Make sure your subscription or product license is active.
6. Restart Office.
